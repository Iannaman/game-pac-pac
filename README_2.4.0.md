# PAC PAC 2.4.0 - Arcade scoring

- Combo window: 1.25 seconds; multiplier rises every 10 consecutive collectibles, from x1 to x8.
- Losing a life or letting the timer expire resets the combo.
- Power ghost chain awards 200, 400, 800 and 1,600 points in the same power window.
- Near miss threshold: 0.65 to 0.86 cells, two-second cooldown per ghost, disabled during power or invulnerability.
- Live combo HUD with countdown bar.
- End-of-round arcade breakdown and grade D-S.
- Separate collectible combo statistics for Giovanni and Bruno in multiplayer.

Grades are based on final score, highest multiplier, near misses and lives lost. Gameplay speed, collision radii and difficulty settings are unchanged. QR, WhatsApp sharing, signaling and performance protections remain included.
